# Official-Rules Facts

Source: https://hackathon.nerdy.com/terms

Version published: 2026-08-27

Captured: 2026-09-04

## Highest-impact sections

- Section 3: eligible adults in listed jurisdictions, including India; individual
  entries only; collaborators require rights assignments.
- Section 4: working learning product, English description, functioning demo no
  longer than three minutes, and material/AI disclosures; one entry per person.
- Section 5: no substantive changes after close.
- Section 6: human judges, threshold review, no mandatory public rubric, no duty
  to run the product, and no guaranteed award.
- Section 7: broad assignment of submission ownership to Nerdy; non-commercial
  license back; third-party and AI disclosure; prohibited copyleft licenses;
  privacy, biometric, and child-testing restrictions.
- Section 8: prize verification, tax documentation, payment timing, and entrant
  responsibility for taxes and fees.
- Section 9: five-year marketing rights and possible Demo Day recording.
- Section 11: entry and prizes are separate from hiring and provide no interview
  or employment guarantee.
- Section 13.3: official rules control over conflicting challenge statements.

## Product-development consequences

- Maintain an exact dependency and asset ledger from day one.
- Use synthetic learner data by default.
- Require verifiable parental consent for any data from a child under 13.
- Keep deterministic arithmetic validation separate from generative output.
- Do not mix pre-existing commercial IP into the submission casually.
- Do not add a broad public/open-source license without reviewing the ownership
  assignment and planned reuse.
- Treat the demo video and written description as primary judging surfaces.
