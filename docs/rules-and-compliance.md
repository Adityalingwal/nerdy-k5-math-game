# Rules and Compliance

This is a working checklist, not legal advice. It summarizes the official rules
published at https://hackathon.nerdy.com/terms on 2026-08-27 and verified on
2026-09-04, then live rechecked on 2026-09-07. Re-read the live rules before
submission; the official rules control over all other challenge statements.

## Entry constraints

- Entrant must be an eligible individual, at least 18 and the age of majority.
- India is an eligible jurisdiction.
- The entry is individual, not a team entry. Any collaborator must assign all
  relevant rights to the entrant in writing.
- Only one entry per person is considered; the most recent valid submission
  replaces earlier ones.
- Work must be created or substantially developed during the entry period. We
  must state what was developed during that period.
- All submission material must be in English or include an English translation.
- The working entry must remain freely accessible to Nerdy without restriction
  through the judging period.
- After the deadline, substantive changes are not allowed.

## Demo and evaluation constraints

- Demo video must show the entry functioning and must not exceed three minutes.
- Judges are not required to watch past three minutes.
- Judges are not required to install, execute, or test the entry.
- There is no guaranteed public scoring methodology and no obligation to award a
  prize if entries are judged insufficient.

## AI and third-party material

- Generative-AI assistance is expressly allowed and encouraged.
- The entrant must be able to explain the submitted work and must disclose the
  nature and extent of AI assistance.
- Every open-source component, license, API, dataset, model, font, image, and
  audio asset must be disclosed.
- Do not use GPL, LGPL, AGPL, SSPL, or other reciprocal/copyleft components.
- Confirm that every third-party asset permits the rights Nerdy requires.

## Child safety and data

- Do not develop or test with real student data.
- Do not test with data from anyone under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
- Do not include third-party personal information or depict an identifiable
  person other than the entrant.
- Do not collect biometric identifiers or perform face, voiceprint, emotion,
  attention, or affect inference. Non-identifying speech recognition,
  transcription, and pronunciation assessment are permitted.
- For our math game, default to synthetic learner profiles and anonymous event
  data. Any child usability session needs documented parental consent and must
  avoid retaining identifying media or data.

## Ownership warning

Submitting the entry irrevocably assigns Nerdy worldwide ownership of the entry
and its intellectual-property rights, subject to the exact terms in Section 7.
Nerdy licenses the entry back to the entrant perpetually for **non-commercial**
use, including continued development and portfolio display.

This materially affects reuse and commercialization. Do not add a public license,
reuse pre-existing proprietary code, accept collaborator code, or submit valuable
unrelated platform IP without separately reviewing the ownership consequences.

## Publicity and employment

- Nerdy receives broad marketing rights over the entry and the entrant's name,
  likeness, voice, city, country, and challenge statements for five years;
  previously published material may remain in circulation.
- Demo Day may be recorded.
- Entry, finalist status, and winning do not create a right to an interview,
  employment offer, or consulting engagement.

## Practical operating interpretation

This section records how we will work under the rules. It does not replace the
official rules, provide legal advice, or lock the product scope.

- **One accountable entrant:** Submit in Aditya's name only after eligibility,
  residency, exclusions, and conflicting obligations are reconfirmed. AI tools
  assist development; they are not co-entrants.
- **Human contributions need clean rights:** Do not incorporate human-authored
  code, design, art, audio, or content until we know whether written assignment
  is required. Licensed third-party material remains a separate licensing case.
- **Preserve entry-period provenance:** Keep dated decisions, commits, tests,
  demo evidence, and disclosures for pre-existing material. Do not assume that
  cosmetic changes to an older product count as substantial development.
- **Prepare a complete submission package:** Plan for a working learning product,
  an English description covering what it does, how it was built, and what comes
  next, a functioning demo video no longer than three minutes, and complete
  disclosures. A repository, live deployment, and supporting material are
  optional under the official rules unless the submission form later requires
  them.
- **Verify access and freeze the judged build:** Before the deadline, test access
  while signed out, verify hosting and API limits, capture the release record,
  and stop substantive changes. Keep the entry accessible through the required
  judging period.
- **Treat disclosure and permission separately:** Disclose both builder-side and
  product-runtime generative AI, plus every dependency, model, API, dataset,
  font, image, audio asset, and other third-party component. Audit permission for
  every item and reject GPL, LGPL, AGPL, SSPL, and other reciprocal/copyleft
  components.
- **Use synthetic learner data:** Demo with adults following scripted learner
  profiles and synthetic or anonymous temporary events. Do not collect a child's
  name, face, voice, camera or microphone data, or biometric/affect inference.
  Test with anyone under 13 only after a documented compliant parental-consent
  process, without retaining identifying information.
- **Keep submitted IP bounded:** Build a hackathon-specific entry and avoid
  mixing in unrelated reusable proprietary platform IP. Do not publicly license
  the entry before reviewing the ownership clause. Any ambiguity about what
  counts as the submitted Entry needs written clarification from Nerdy or
  qualified legal advice.
- **Design for human evaluation, not guaranteed outcomes:** Clear compliance and
  a convincing three-minute learning story matter because the rules publish no
  guaranteed scoring rubric or testing obligation. Winning, interviews, and
  employment remain goals, not claims or guarantees.
