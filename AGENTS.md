# Repository Instructions

These instructions apply to every coding or research agent working in this
repository.

1. Read `docs/00-index.md` before making recommendations or edits.
2. Treat `docs/rules-and-compliance.md` as a hard constraint. The official rules
   override landing-page copy and all agent assumptions.
3. Keep verified facts, product hypotheses, decisions, and open questions
   explicitly separate.
4. Do not lock or expand product scope without recording the reason in
   `docs/decision-log.md`.
5. Keep the submission centered on one learner, one math misconception, one
   repeatable learning loop, and one clear AI contribution.
6. Do not use real student data. Testing with anyone under 13 requires verifiable
   parental consent. Do not store a child's name, face, voice, or other personal
   information.
7. Do not add GPL, LGPL, AGPL, SSPL, or other reciprocal/copyleft components.
   Record every dependency, model, API, dataset, font, image, audio asset, and
   generative-AI contribution in `docs/evidence-register.md`.
8. After material work, update `docs/progress.md`. Add evidence for every claim
   that may appear in the submission or demo.
9. Winning, finalist selection, interviews, and employment are goals, not claims
   or guarantees.
10. Prefer small verified changes. Do not build platform, LMS, social, account,
    or teacher-dashboard features unless a recorded decision proves they are
    necessary for the core demo.
